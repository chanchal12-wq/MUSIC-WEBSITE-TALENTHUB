# Talent Hub – The Local Artist Network 🎵

Talent Hub is a modern music collaboration and streaming platform designed to empower local artists by giving them a dedicated space to showcase their talent, connect with other musicians, and reach a wider audience through technology.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Many local artists struggle to gain visibility on global music platforms. Talent Hub solves this problem by creating a dedicated platform where independent musicians can upload songs, collaborate with other artists, and interact with listeners.

The platform focuses on:
- Music sharing
- Artist collaboration
- Community engagement
- Talent promotion
- Music discovery

---
